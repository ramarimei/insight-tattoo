## Pricing
- **Single Session Tattoos:** Per-design pricing — email with image, placement, and size for a quote
- **Ongoing Sessions:** $140/hour
- **Full Day Bookings:** $800 (approx 7hrs)
- **Deposit:** $100 booking fee required (non-refundable), secures appointment time
- **Custom Design Fee:** Applied for custom artwork (up to 8hrs for large designs, 2hrs for small)
- **Cancellation Policy:** 48 hours notice required. Cancellations within 48hrs require deposit to be paid again.
